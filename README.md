# Shahab Mafi

Vehicle energy and emissions research. I turn real-world driving data into models that hold up under scrutiny.

## What I work on

**Driving cycles from telematics.** Thousands of OBD-II trips logged second by second, segmented into microtrips and recombined into cycles that reproduce how a fleet actually drives.

**Similarity that can be demonstrated.** Most studies test whether two sets of driving differ, which cannot show they are alike. I use energy distance and equivalence testing (TOST), which can.

**Engine simulation.** One-dimensional GT-Power models calibrated against measured data. Around 190 models across nine projects: heavy-duty diesel, gasoline direct injection, marine outboard, gas-fuelled engines, supercharger matching, and an EV with supercapacitor storage.

**Energy and emissions modelling.** Vehicle energy consumption, well-to-tank and tank-to-wheel analysis, life-cycle assessment.

## Tools

Python (pandas, NumPy, SciPy, Matplotlib) · GT-Power / GT-SUITE · MATLAB and Simulink · FMU / Modelica · COPERT · MOVES

## Background

PhD research at Iran University of Science and Technology. Alongside it, four years as an ECU calibration engineer at IPCO (Irankhodro Powertrain) — it is one thing to model an engine, another to make a real one behave.
